This year's theme: OPEN UP

MOVE is Kirkeibyen's annual conference, where we gather across our locations. It is a weekend of community, vision and time together as a church.

We meet to share life together, strengthen relationships and be inspired by what we are called to do as a community.

MOVE is not about one location — but about all of Kirkeibyen coming together as one church.
