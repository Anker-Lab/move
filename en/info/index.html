Guest Speaker

Mark Foster

Pastor & Preacher from Audacious Church, UK

Mark Foster is a dynamic preacher and leader from Audacious Church in England – a church known for its strong missional culture, young expression and significant influence on churches across the UK over the past many years.

At MOVE26, he will join Kirkeibyen's pastors in sharing teaching and inspiration based on this year's theme OPEN UP.

Theme OPEN UP
Date September 11–12

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MOVE?+
MOVE is Kirkeibyen's annual conference, where we come together for community, worship, teaching and inspiration across all our locations.
Can I attend if I'm new?+
Yes. MOVE is for both newcomers and those who already know the church, and it is a great way to experience the community.
Can I participate if I don't speak Danish?+
Yes. Live English translation is available during the main sessions.
What is included in the price?+

Lunch and dinner on Saturday are included in the price.

Coffee and tea are free throughout the conference, as well as the sweet treats served with afternoon coffee on Saturday.
There will be an additional charge if you use the café after the evening service.

Can I stay in a caravan during the conference?+
If you would like to stay in a caravan during the conference, you can contact us for more information. There will be an additional charge for electricity and other facilities.
Is there a church service on Sunday morning?+

There is a church service on Sunday morning at our churches in Kolding, Fredericia and Haderslev.

The service is free, and everyone is welcome.

Who should I contact?+
You can contact the MOVE team via the link at the bottom of the page.
