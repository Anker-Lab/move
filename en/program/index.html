WORKSHOP

Karen Harvey

Karen Harvey will talk about how we can develop a healthier mind and thought life by opening up to good thoughts and emotions and shutting out the negative ones. A practical elective with tools for a healthy inner life.

WORKSHOP

Kamilla Seerup

Kamilla Seerup is hosting a workshop on navigating life's choices. With experience from both her own life and her work with young people, she will provide tools to help you find your way through important decisions.
