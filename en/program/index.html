FOR FAMILIES

For the little ones

If your child is too young for Mini Church, you are very welcome to stay in the café. There are toys and a TV, so you can be with your child while still following the program.

Friday

18:00
ARRIVAL

Check-in

Welcome to MOVE! You can check in at the main entrance of Samværket.

19:00
OPENING MEETING

Session 1 · Mark Foster

We kick off MOVE with a bang as our guest speaker Mark Foster opens the conference.

21:00
COMMUNITY

Café

Come by for a cozy time, community and something good to eat. The café is run by Believe from Kildevæld, who are traveling to share the Word of God. All profits go towards their trip to Uganda.

Session 2 · Jesper Kure

MEETING

Session 3 · Mark Foster

Mark Foster will share a message inviting us to let God transform our lives.

Q&A

Electives

BREAK

Coffee & Community

ACTIVITIES

Afternoon Activities

Spend the afternoon enjoying activities, community and a relaxed atmosphere. Choose between several different activities:

🎲 Board Games Games and fun in the café
🏆 Ladder Golf & Pétanque Outdoor activities in front of Samværket
🚶 Walk A relaxing walk starting at the main entrance

Dinner

Session 4 · Mark Foster

We finish MOVE with a powerful evening together with Mark Foster. Join us for worship, community and a message from God.

COMMUNITY

Sunday

Sunday Service
