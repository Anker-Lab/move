WORKSHOP

Karen Harvey

Karen Harvey will talk about how we can develop a healthier mind and thought life by opening ourselves up to good thoughts and feelings and shutting out the negative ones. A practical elective with tools for a healthy inner life.

WORKSHOP

Kamilla Seerup

Kamilla Seerup is hosting a workshop about navigating life's choices. Drawing on experience from both her own life and her work with young people, she will provide tools to help you find your way through important decisions.
