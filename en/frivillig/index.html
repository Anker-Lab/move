Volunteer

Become part of the MOVE team

MOVE is created by volunteers. Whether you love people, practical tasks, creativity or good coffee, there is a place for you. Choose the area that suits you best and help create an amazing weekend.

Sign up as a volunteer

Hosting Team

Create a warm welcome for all participants. Help with information and guest services, and make sure people feel seen and well taken care of.

Practical Team

Help make things work behind the scenes. Set-up, take-down and practical tasks before, during and after the conference.

Creative Team

Worship, sound, lighting, video, photography and media. Help create experiences and communicate what is happening at MOVE.

Children & Youth

Create a safe and fun environment for children and young people through activities, relationships and teaching.

Café Team

Brew coffee, serve snacks and help create welcoming spaces throughout the weekend.

Decor Team

Help with decorations, atmosphere and details to give MOVE a welcoming and cohesive look.

Food Team

Help prepare, serve and coordinate meals so participants can focus on community and the program.
